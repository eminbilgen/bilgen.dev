Questions

Before you write.

Will the page rank or convert for sure?

No one can promise that. I build the technical and structural foundations that help: speed, mobile layout, structured data, clear calls to action and tracking, so you can see what works.

Do you write the copy?

I can draft it with you from what you tell me about the business, and you approve everything. I do not invent reviews, numbers or claims.

Can you host it?

Yes. I run my own servers, or I can deliver the files so you host them anywhere.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope. Send me what you do and where your customers come from, and I will reply with a plan, a price and a date.
