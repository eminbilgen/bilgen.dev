Symptoms

Sound familiar?

Mail lands in spam

Messages from your domain arrive in junk at Gmail, Outlook or Yahoo, or only some recipients see them.

Bounces and rejections

Senders get errors like "SPF fail", "DMARC policy" or "relay denied", or your own mail is refused.

Someone is spoofing you

Fake mail goes out in your name because nothing tells receivers to reject it.

Mail broke after a change

A new host, a new mailer or a certificate swap and suddenly some devices, or some recipients, stop working.

What I check

The whole chain, not one record.

Authentication

  • SPF: correct senders, the 10-lookup limit, one record only
  • DKIM: selectors present, key length (2048-bit), signing actually on
  • DMARC: policy, alignment and reporting address

Server identity

  • MX and the mail host's real behaviour
  • Reverse DNS and HELO matching the sending IP
  • TLS certificate valid and matching the mail hostname

Transport security

  • MTA-STS and TLS-RPT where they make sense
  • Port and protocol setup for IMAP and SMTP clients

Reputation

  • Blocklist status of the sending IP
  • Real test messages to the major providers, with headers read line by line

The free mailcheck tool covers the DNS part in seconds. The paid work is what it cannot do: reading the server, fixing it and proving the result.

How it works

Audit, fix, verify.

  1. 01

  2. 02

    Findings

  3. 03

  4. 04

    I resend test mail and show the passing results, so you see the change instead of taking my word.

Questions

Before you write.

Can you guarantee my email reaches the inbox?

No one honestly can. Inbox placement also depends on your sending reputation and content. What I can do is remove every technical reason mail gets rejected or flagged, and show you the evidence.

Do you need access to my mailboxes?

No. The audit uses public DNS records and test messages. I only need DNS access, or I give you the exact records to paste, and server access if the mail server itself needs changes.

Which mail setups do you work with?

Self-hosted Postfix and Dovecot servers, and domains sending through services such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or a transactional relay.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of domains and whether the mail server needs changes. Send me your domain and I will reply with a plan, a price and a date.
