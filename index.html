About

One person, from first sketch to server.

I'm Emin, an independent web engineer based in Ankara. Since 2019 I've been building websites and the infrastructure underneath them, and I've worked for myself since 2021.

I work alone on purpose. You talk to the person who designs it, writes it and keeps it running, so nothing gets lost between a designer, a developer and a sysadmin.

Process

How a project runs.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You tell me what you are building and who it is for. A short message is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Plan

    I reply with an approach, a price and a date, so you can decide before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Build

    You see a working version early on a private link and keep seeing it as it grows.

  4. 04

    Launch & run

    I put it live and can keep hosting and maintaining it, or hand everything over cleanly.

Questions

What kind of projects do you take?

Marketing and portfolio sites with real interaction, web apps with a back end and admin panel, payment and checkout flows, and the hosting, mail and DNS around them.

Yes. I run my own servers, so I can host, monitor and maintain what I build, or deliver it so you can host it anywhere.

Will my work stay private?

By default, yes. I don't list clients or show unreleased work without permission.
